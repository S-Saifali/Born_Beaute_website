The studio

Quiet rooms, plain advice, work that grows out well.

Wren Adeyemi opened Born Beauté in 2013 with two chairs and a rule that still holds: nobody leaves with a look they cannot manage themselves. Colour is mixed in the room, skin is read before it is treated, and the shop only carries what the team uses on the floor.

Certified specialists

Every colourist and aesthetician licensed and re-trained yearly.

Premium products

Ammonia-free colour lines and fragrance-free skincare only.

Unhurried rooms

One guest per stylist, so nobody is left under a timer.

Flexible booking

Evenings until nine, free reschedule up to 24 hours ahead.
