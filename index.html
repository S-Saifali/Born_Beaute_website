The studio

Full-service beauty, done properly.

Born Beauté is a full-service beauty studio in Arlington Heights. We specialise in every type of hair colouring, women’s haircuts, styling and treatments — alongside makeup, manicures, pedicures, brows and lash lifts. The focus is high-quality technique, personalised service, and a warm, welcoming experience for every client. Proud to be Google Rated 2025.

Experienced team

The team

Nine specialists, one studio.

Colourists, stylists, nail artists and a brow & lash specialist — each with their own craft and years behind the chair.

Halyna Pylypchuk

Hairdresser & Colourist · 13+ yrs

A hairdresser with more than 13 years’ experience who loves to create beauty. Halyna works across dyeing techniques tailored to your wishes while keeping the look luxurious, and only ever uses high-quality materials and tools. Attentive to detail, she believes good hair adds confidence.

Olya Miciu

Hair Extension Specialist · 10+ yrs

A professional hairstylist with over 10 years in the industry, specialising in natural-looking transformations with a focus on hair health — hand-tied KOVI extensions, balayage, keratin, hair botox and nanoplasty. Every extension service begins with a consultation to customise colour, length and placement.

Olena

Master Hairdresser · 16 yrs

A master hairdresser with 16 years’ experience across women’s, men’s and children’s haircuts. Olena believes the right cut changes a person and emphasises their individuality, and will choose the colour, care and styling to complete a unique look.

Diana Moseichuck

Lash & Brow Artist

A lash & brow artist who genuinely loves her craft — all types of lash extensions, lash lifts, brow lamination, shaping and tinting. Diana values precision, natural-yet-beautiful results and a personalised approach, so every client leaves feeling more confident.

Luda Oliinyk

Hair Stylist · 6 yrs

A stylist with six years’ experience, countless styles and one mission: turning hair into art. Luda brings creativity, precision and a touch of magic to every look.

Polina Lutitskaia

Hairdresser & Colourist · 18 yrs

A hairdresser and colourist with 18 years in the industry, specialising in women’s haircuts and colour of all levels of complexity. Polina combines modern technique with a strong focus on hair health, shape and results customised to each client.

Olya Garasymchuk

Nail Artist

A nail artist passionate about helping clients feel confident and beautiful through quality nail care. Olya is dedicated to a relaxing experience with close attention to detail, cleanliness and comfort — from a classic manicure to a fresh new set.

Anna Skopych

Top Nail Artist · 10+ yrs

A highly skilled nail artist with over 10 years’ experience. Originally from Kyiv, where she owned and ran her own studio, Anna specialises in elegant designs, structured gel overlays and natural nail care, always focused on the health and beauty of her clients’ nails.

Andréa Miléo

Makeup Artist

A makeup artist specialising in soft glam and flawless, natural-looking beauty — enhancing your glow for every special moment, from everyday looks to bridal, photoshoots and prom.
