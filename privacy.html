Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how Born Beauté Salon (“we”, “us”) handles information collected through this website. We keep what we collect to a minimum and never sell it.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: if you send us a message, we receive the name, email and message you provide, so we can reply.
  • Usage data: if analytics is enabled, we collect anonymous, aggregated data (pages viewed, device type, general location) to understand how the site is used. This does not identify you personally.
  • We do not take bookings, payments or gift-card orders on this site — those happen on Square (see below).

How your message is delivered

Our contact form is delivered by Web3Forms, which forwards your message to our studio email. Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry.

Booking, payments & gift cards

Appointments, payments and gift-card purchases are handled by Square on their own hosted pages. Any information you enter there is governed by Square’s privacy policy, not ours.

Maps & embedded content

Our contact section embeds a Google Map. Google may collect data in accordance with its privacy policy.

Marketing

If you contact us, we may occasionally send you news or offers. You can opt out at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or letting us know at your next visit.

Cookies

This site uses only what’s needed to function, plus analytics cookies if analytics is enabled. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Just get in touch and we’ll take care of it.
